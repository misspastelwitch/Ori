This portfolio presents a selection of recent works, installations, sculptures, drawings, and ongoing projects. My practice moves between mythology, grotesque embodiment, transformation, and emotional worldbuilding, often combining multiple disciplines and materials within a single body of work. My projects often move from drawing to painting and sculpture. My sketchbooks are the starting point, filled with details, ideas, and visual comparisons.
