Ori van Gelderen is a visual artist working with painting, ceramics, and mixed media. Her work explores female sexuality, myth, and bodily transformation through figurative Imagery and sculptural forms. Drawing on folklore, feminist thought, and personal experience, she creates symbolic worlds where women, animals, and hybrid creatures reflect themes of desire, power, rage, and vulnerability. Her practice moves between drawing, painting, and sculpture, allowing characters and narratives to evolve across materials.
