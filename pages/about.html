About
me

I am Ori (Victoria) van Gelderen, a Dutch multidisciplinary artist based in Antwerp. My work explores mythology, transformation, grotesque embodiment, and emotional worldbuilding through installation, sculpture, ceramics, painting, textiles, and mixed media.

Drawing from feminist theory, Jewish mysticism, folklore, bodily experience, and contemporary visual culture, I create immersive environments inhabited by creatures, masks, symbolic figures, and shifting forms. These worlds exist somewhere between dream and ritual, beauty and horror, vulnerability and resistance.

At the heart of my practice is a fascination with transformation. Through recurring themes of femininity, monstrosity, emotional truth, and bodily experience, I investigate how personal and cultural narratives shape the way we inhabit our bodies and imagine ourselves.

Rather than offering fixed meanings, my work invites viewers into symbolic environments where contradiction can exist openly and where multiple interpretations are possible. Through artistic worldbuilding, I create spaces that function as emotional ecosystems: places where fear, tenderness, grief, desire, comfort, and transformation can coexist.
